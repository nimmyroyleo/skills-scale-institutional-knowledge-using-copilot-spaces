# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads help the team turn plans into predictable outcomes by coordinating day-to-day execution and removing delivery blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Track progress, dependencies, and impediments across the delivery team
- Coordinate handoffs between product, development, QA, and release activities
- Escalate risks and decisions to the Project Manager
- Facilitate delivery check-ins and support continuous improvement

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and flow
- Resolve blockers before they affect commitments
- Make ownership and handoffs visible

### Typical Communication
- Delivery boards and dependency trackers
- Daily execution updates and escalation notes
- Retrospective actions and follow-ups

### Role Interactions
- Partner with the Project Manager on schedules, risks, and status reporting
- Coordinate with the Product Manager to sequence prioritized work
- Help Developers and QA resolve blockers and manage handoffs
- Keep Stakeholders informed of delivery risks without changing product priorities

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical decisions and implementation approaches so that the solution is secure, maintainable, and aligned with product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approaches and document significant decisions
- Break down complex work and identify technical dependencies
- Review designs and code for quality, security, and maintainability
- Surface technical risks, constraints, and trade-offs early

### Goals
- Deliver a coherent and sustainable technical solution
- Reduce technical risk and rework
- Enable Developers to make consistent implementation decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and decision records
- Architecture and code reviews
- Technical risk updates in planning and status meetings

### Role Interactions
- Translate Product Manager goals into feasible technical options
- Support Developers with design guidance while preserving team ownership
- Coordinate with QA on testability, quality risks, and acceptance coverage
- Advise the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and technical risks
- Explain technical trade-offs to Stakeholders in clear, outcome-focused terms

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate readiness, approvals, and communications for moving completed work safely into production.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release plans, checklists, and go/no-go criteria
- Coordinate validation, approvals, and deployment dependencies
- Track release risks, rollback plans, and post-release checks
- Communicate release status and outcomes to affected teams

### Goals
- Deliver controlled, low-risk releases
- Make release readiness and ownership transparent
- Detect and respond to production issues quickly

### Typical Communication
- Release calendars and readiness checklists
- Go/no-go decisions and deployment announcements
- Post-release validation and incident summaries

### Role Interactions
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on milestones, risks, and stakeholder updates
- Confirm scope and launch outcomes with the Product Manager
- Work with Developers and QA to verify build, test, and rollback readiness
- Partner with Support / Operations Representatives on monitoring and user communications
- Notify Stakeholders about timing, impact, and release outcomes

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers make product experiences clear, accessible, and useful by representing user needs throughout discovery and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and map key journeys
- Create flows, wireframes, prototypes, and interaction specifications
- Validate designs with users and incorporate actionable feedback
- Define usability and accessibility considerations for acceptance

### Goals
- Reduce user friction and improve task completion
- Ensure solutions are usable and accessible
- Give the team shared clarity about the intended experience

### Typical Communication
- User journey maps and research findings
- Wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Design reviews and usability feedback

### Role Interactions
- Partner with the Product Manager on user problems, outcomes, and priorities
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to make designs feasible
- Coordinate with QA on usability and accessibility checks
- Help the Project Manager plan design activities and dependencies
- Use Stakeholder feedback to validate direction without replacing user evidence

---

## Data Analysts

### Role Summary
Data Analysts turn product and delivery data into actionable insights for prioritization, measurement, and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics and reporting approaches with clear assumptions
- Analyze product usage, quality, and delivery trends
- Maintain trustworthy dashboards and document data definitions
- Share findings, limitations, and recommended actions

### Goals
- Support decisions with reliable evidence
- Make outcomes and success metrics visible
- Identify opportunities to improve product and delivery performance

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and analysis notes
- Dashboards and outcome reports
- Experiment readouts and retrospective insights

### Role Interactions
- Collaborate with the Product Manager to define success metrics and evaluate outcomes
- Provide the Project Manager with delivery trends and forecasting signals
- Help Developers and QA interpret telemetry, defects, and quality trends
- Clarify evidence and trade-offs for Stakeholders
- Work with UX Designers when behavioral data complements user research

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Operations Representatives bring operational and customer-facing knowledge into planning, releases, and ongoing service improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Represent support cases, operational constraints, and service health needs
- Prepare support guidance, runbooks, and escalation paths
- Monitor releases for customer impact and recurring issues
- Feed incidents and user feedback into prioritization and retrospectives

### Goals
- Maintain a reliable and supportable service
- Reduce customer disruption and time to resolution
- Ensure operational feedback informs product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Support queues, incident channels, and escalation paths
- Runbooks, release notes, and knowledge-base updates
- Service health and post-incident reviews

### Role Interactions
- Partner with the Project Manager on operational risks, dependencies, and communications
- Share customer impact and support trends with the Product Manager
- Coordinate with Developers and QA on reproduction, verification, and remediation
- Work with the Release Manager on readiness, monitoring, and rollback communications
- Keep Stakeholders informed about service impact and recovery progress
